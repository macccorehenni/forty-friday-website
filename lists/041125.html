Set 5— On sonic layering

Several songs, including the first in this set, Unite by Juniper Vale, have excellent sonic layering. Kid and Leveret and Unite both have portions that are two voices just over each other. It lends a gravitas that makes any song much more amazing.

  • 26: Unite by Juniper Vale (indie pop)
  • 25: Journey by Snail's House (kawaii future bass)
